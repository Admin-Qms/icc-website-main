Your path to certification

From first gap to passed audit, in one system

1

Know where you stand

A certified consultant walks the floor for the gap analysis and scores every clause. The Standards module then shows the status of each clause and the evidence behind it, so you see your gaps before the auditor does.

2

Set up your system

Documents holds your procedures, Processes holds the process maps and their owners, and Training builds the competency matrix. AI drafting, if you turn it on, helps with first versions, and a person reviews each one.

3

Work as usual, records build themselves

Audits, Improvements, Safety, Assets, Vendors and Supplier Quality produce the records as the work happens, with overdue items flagged on your dashboard.

4

Walk into the audit ready

The statement of applicability, audit reports and sealed CAPA records are ready for the certification body. If you want a consultant in the room for Stage 1 and Stage 2, they are there.

What you get

Less manual work, and early warning before anything slips

An AI assistant, optional AI drafting, notifications, a home dashboard and a consultant on call. AI drafts when you turn it on, notifications and the dashboard flag what needs attention, and a person reviews and signs off.

AI Assistant

Carries out tasks in the software within the permissions of the person asking. A multi-step request becomes a visible plan the user approves, and destructive steps need extra confirmation.

Notifications and daily summary

Email notifications when work is assigned or due, and a daily summary of pending work, so nothing waits in someone's inbox unnoticed.

Optional AI drafting

AI drafting of controlled documents is off until the organization turns it on. Every draft enters the normal review and approval lifecycle before it is published.

Home dashboard

An attention-required panel and a coverage overview on sign-in, with My actions, email notifications and a daily summary of pending work behind it.

Consultant On Call

The software holds the evidence; a certified lead consultant reviews the work and joins you for the audit that matters.

How AI is kept in check

AI does the paperwork. You stay in control.

You will always know who did what. The assistant works inside the permissions you already set, shows its plan before it acts, and leaves a trail that separates AI from people. Gap assessments and audits are always done by people. As AI compliance software, it prepares drafts only, and a person reviews and signs off on each one.

Acts within your permissions

The assistant can only do what the person asking is allowed to do.

Plans before it acts

A multi-step request becomes a visible plan that the user approves first.

Confirms destructive steps

Anything that deletes or overwrites data needs extra confirmation.

Drafting is off until you turn it on

AI drafting of documents is an organization-level setting, off by default.

A person always signs off

Drafts produced with AI are suggestions. A person reviews and accepts each one before it enters a controlled record.
