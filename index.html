How it works

How you get certified, in six steps

You always know where you are and what comes next. Consultants run the gap analysis and audits with you, and the software keeps the evidence from day one.

  1. Step 01Weeks 1–2

    Gap Analysis

    Find out exactly what you are missing, clause by clause, with an owner for every gap.

  2. Step 02Weeks 2–5

    System Design

    Your processes are mapped as they really run, and the clauses that apply are marked.

  3. Step 03Weeks 4–9

    Documentation

    Procedures and work instructions are drafted, with optional AI help and expert review.

  4. Step 04Weeks 8–16

    Implementation

    Your team is trained, and the software tracks who still owes what.

  5. Step 05Weeks 14–18

    Internal Audit

    A full internal audit and management review catch problems early, while there is time to fix them.

  6. Step 06Weeks 18–24

    Certification

    Support through your Stage 1 and Stage 2 audits, then help staying ready for surveillance audits.

How AI helps

AI does the paperwork. You stay in control.

AI works only within the permissions you set. It prepares drafts and handles routine tasks, and nothing becomes an official record until a person approves it. Gap assessments and audits are always done by people.

How the software is kept in check

Acts within your permissions

The assistant can only do what the person asking is allowed to do.

Shows its plan first

For a multi-step request, you see the plan and approve it before anything happens.

Asks before deleting

Anything that deletes or overwrites data needs your extra confirmation.

AI drafting is your choice

Document drafting is off until your organization turns it on.

A person always signs off

AI drafts are suggestions until someone on your team reviews and accepts them.
